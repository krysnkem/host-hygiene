# host hygiene: brand

**Name:** host hygiene. Command: `hh`. Mascot: Hy.
**Line:** Reclaim your Mac. Only when you say so.
**One sentence:** Finds what eats disk, memory and CPU on a Mac, explains each item, and clears only what you approve.

## Voice
Calm, plain, a little warm. A careful colleague, not a cleaner app. Say what will happen before it happens, in the user's words ("free 4.2 GB", not "purge cache"). Never alarm: a low disk is a fact with a next step. Never promise what the tool does not do. The one promise it makes loudly: **it never closes, kills or signals anything.**

Write: "Nothing was removed." "I could not read the machine just now. Press r to try again."
Avoid: exclamation marks, "optimize", "boost", "junk", "supercharge", fear language about speed.

## Hy
A rounded square with two eyes and a mouth, drawn from the terminal face `(•ᴗ•)`. Six moods, each tied to a state: calm (default), working, done, tight (a resource is low), stop (critical), blink (idle). Hy speaks in first person, briefly, and only in the guided look. Never animate Hy outside the terminal's own blink. Files: `hy-mark.svg`, `hy-moods.svg`.

## Type
Display: Bricolage Grotesque (700, tight tracking). Body: Instrument Sans. Data, commands, the terminal: IBM Plex Mono. All on Google Fonts; fallbacks Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue, Menlo.

## Logo
`logo.svg` (light) and `logo-dark.svg`: Hy beside the lowercase name, with `hh · for your Mac` beneath. Clear space equal to Hy's eye gap on every side. Minimum width 120 px. The wordmark text is live type in the SVGs: export to outlines before using it where the fonts are not installed. `social.svg` is the 1280 x 640 share image.

## Never
Use Hy on a red or amber ground. Stretch or rotate the mark. Put the name in title case in running text (it is `host hygiene`, lowercase, two words). Imply the tool is made by or endorsed by Apple: say "for your Mac", and do not use the Apple logo or a Mac illustration.
